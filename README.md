# 🏏 Rohit Sharma — Power BI Cricket Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** Analyzing The International Cricket Career of **Rohit Sharma**, With a Focus On Career Performance, Centuries, Match Impact, And Statistical Trends.

This project Demonstrates Practical **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** Skills Using Power BI, Power Query, DAX, And Structured Cricket Datasets.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The Main Objectives Of This Project Are To:

* Analyze Rohit Sharma's international Cricket Career
* Track career Performance And Major Milestones
* Analyze The Distribution And Timeline Of Centuries
* Understand Batting Performance across different periods
* Explore the Relationship Between individual Performances And Match Outcomes
* Build An Interactive and Visually Clear Power BI Dashboard

---

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Career Overview

Provides a High-level summary of Rohit Sharma's Career Statistics And Major Performance Indicators.

Key areas include:

* Career Runs
* Matches Played
* Batting Performance
* Centuries
* Major Career Milestones
* Overall Performance Trends

---

### 2. Century Timeline

Analyzes Rohit Sharma's International Centuries Over His Career.

The page focuses on:

* Century count
* Century Timeline
* Performance Trends
* Major Scoring Milestones
* Distribution Of centuries Across His Career

---

### 3. Win Impact

Explores How Major Batting Performances Relate To Match Outcomes.

The Analysis Includes:

* Performance In winning matches
* Performance In other match outcomes
* Runs Scored
* Century Performances
* Match-result Relationships
